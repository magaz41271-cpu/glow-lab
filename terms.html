Glow Lab Studio

Terms of Service & Privacy Policy · Effective November 12, 2026

Glow Lab Studio is an internal content tool operated by Glow Lab Media. It helps our own team prepare product review videos and upload them as drafts to TikTok accounts that we own, for manual review before anything is published.

Terms of Service

  1. Who may use it. The app is used only by Glow Lab Media staff and the TikTok accounts we operate. It is not offered to the general public.
  2. What it does. With your authorization, the app uploads videos to your TikTok inbox as drafts and reads basic profile information (display name, avatar) to show which account is connected. Nothing is published automatically; you finish and publish each video inside the TikTok app.
  3. Your responsibilities. You keep control of your TikTok account and are responsible for the content you publish, including disclosure of paid or affiliate relationships as required by TikTok rules and applicable law (for example the FTC endorsement guidelines).
  4. Revoking access. You can disconnect the app at any time in TikTok → Settings → Security → Manage app permissions, or by asking us to remove the connection.
  5. No warranty. The app is provided as is. We may change or stop it at any time.
  6. Contact. Questions about these terms: see the contact section below.

Privacy Policy

  • Data we access. Through TikTok's Login Kit and Content Posting API we receive an access token, your open ID, display name and avatar URL, and the status of uploads we create. We do not access your private messages, followers, or other users' data.
  • How we use it. Only to identify the connected account and to upload your own videos as drafts. We do not sell, rent, or share this data with third parties, and we do not use it for advertising.
  • Where it is stored. Tokens are stored encrypted-at-rest on a computer controlled by Glow Lab Media and are never transmitted anywhere except to TikTok's API.
  • Retention. Tokens are kept while the account stays connected and deleted within 30 days after you disconnect the app or ask us to remove it.
  • Your rights. You may request a copy or deletion of any data we hold about your account at any time; we respond within 30 days.
  • Children. The app is not intended for anyone under 18.
  • Changes. We will update this page if our practices change; the effective date above reflects the latest version.
